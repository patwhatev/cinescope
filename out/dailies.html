How this reel is cut
Four signal channels score every candidate against your seed films, each normalized independently, then blended by the weights below — your grading is what moves them. A popularity correction then damps films so canonical they sit adjacent to everything, and lifts the deep cuts, since that's where this profile actually lives.
graph — how many seeds converge on it, letterboxd similarity edges
theme — cosine match on letterboxd's mini-theme taxonomy
people — director / writer / cast overlap with your over-represented people
quality — letterboxd rating, shrunk toward the mean by rating count
Seen it — stars are enjoyment. 5★ promotes the film into the seed set at full weight, 4★ at half weight, 3★ is noted, 1–2★ trains the channel weights as a miss.
Not seen — dots are how inclined you are to watch it, not a rating. 2 is "maybe later" (barely trains the weights), 3 is "I'll likely watch it," 4–5 is "I've been meaning to and I know it should be good" — and the training weight climbs with it, from token at 1–2 up to nearly as strong as an actual rating at 5. Still never becomes a seed on its own, though — that still wants a real watch. 3–5 lands on your watchlist. Never counts as a firm miss.
Cards graded before this split show their old star as a dashed hint and count as unreviewed until you answer “seen it?”. Change your mind any time.
Note: letterboxd files a few limited series (Twin Peaks: The Return, Baby Reindeer) under /film/ — pass on those if TV isn't what you're after.
